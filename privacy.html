UZB Strike · oxirgi yangilanish: 2026-yil 6-noyabr

UZB Strike — brauzerda va Android ilovasida ishlaydigan 3D o‘yin. Bu sahifa o‘yin qanday ma’lumotlardan foydalanishini tushuntiradi.

Biz hech qanday shaxsiy ma’lumot yig‘maymiz

  • O‘yinda ro‘yxatdan o‘tish, akkaunt yoki login yo‘q.
  • Ism, telefon raqami, e-pochta, joylashuv, kontaktlar, rasmlar yoki boshqa shaxsiy ma’lumotlar so‘ralmaydi va yig‘ilmaydi.
  • Reklama, analitika yoki kuzatuv (tracking) xizmatlari ishlatilmaydi.
  • Haqiqiy pul bilan to‘lov yo‘q. O‘yindagi pul, keyslar va skinlar faqat o‘yin ichidagi virtual narsalar, ularni sotib olib yoki pulga almashtirib bo‘lmaydi.

Qurilmangizda saqlanadigan ma’lumotlar

O‘yin jarayoni (o‘yin ichidagi pul, inventar, rank, sozlamalar, siz yozgan o‘yinchi ismi) faqat sizning qurilmangizda, brauzer xotirasida (localStorage) saqlanadi. Bu ma’lumotlar bizga yoki boshqa hech kimga yuborilmaydi. Ilovani o‘chirsangiz yoki brauzer ma’lumotlarini tozalasangiz, ular ham o‘chadi.

Reyting jadvalidagi boshqa o‘yinchilar — o‘yin o‘zi yaratadigan sun’iy raqiblar, haqiqiy odamlar emas.

Uchinchi tomon xizmatlari

O‘yin ishlashi uchun quyidagi ochiq manbalardan fayllar yuklanadi. Bunda, har qanday sayt ochilgandagi kabi, qurilmangiz IP manzili va brauzer turi shu xizmatlarga ko‘rinadi:

  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) — o‘yin fayllari shu yerda joylashgan.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare, Inc.) — 3D grafika kutubxonasi (three.js).
  • Google Fonts (Google LLC) — shriftlar.

Biz bu xizmatlardan hech qanday ma’lumot olmaymiz.

Privacy Policy

UZB Strike · last updated November 6, 2026

UZB Strike is a 3D game that runs in the browser and as an Android app.

We do not collect personal data

  • There is no sign-up, account or login.
  • The game does not ask for or collect your name, phone number, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal data.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking services are used.
  • There are no real-money payments. In-game money, cases and skins are virtual items only and cannot be bought with or exchanged for real money.

Data stored on your device

Game progress (in-game money, inventory, rank, settings and the player name you type) is stored only on your device, in browser storage (localStorage). It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing browser data deletes it.

The other players on the leaderboard are AI opponents generated by the game, not real people.

Third-party services

To run, the game loads files from GitHub Pages (game files), cdnjs by Cloudflare (the three.js 3D library) and Google Fonts (fonts). As with any website, these services can see your device's IP address and browser type. We receive no data from them.

Children

The game contains cartoon-style shooting and weapons and is meant for teens and adults, not for children under 13. We collect no data from children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new text will be posted on this page with its update date.
